About Gaianova

Gaianova Multi Service Limited is a modern technology, aviation and renewable energy company focused on creating sustainable engineering solutions for Africa and beyond.

We specialize in aviation consulting, air worthiness management, aviation software management, electric mobility systems, wind energy, charging stations and smart energy infrastructure.
